Solo Tandems

One-car Practice with extended physics for Solo Tandems.
Spawn in pits so you can open setup and teleport back to pits like normal Practice.
Open the Solo Tandems app, record a lead run (or import a .lead), Spawn, set chase car, Start. The lead stays the car the run was recorded in.

Keyboard and controller bindings show in Content Manager under Solo Tandems after the first session starts. They are not listed before that. You do not need to drive.

The lead is visual only — no second physics car. Chase-car teleport needs extended physics (forced on here).
Lap cuts are disarmed so off-track chase lines do not spam “Cut detected”.
